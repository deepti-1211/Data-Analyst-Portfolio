# Supermarket Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing supermarket sales data using Microsoft Excel to identify sales trends, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and revenue patterns.

The objective was to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights that can support better sales monitoring, product planning, and data-driven decision-making.

The project covers the complete data analysis workflow, including data cleaning, data preparation, exploratory analysis, KPI calculation, Pivot Table analysis, and data visualization.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Identify high-performing and low-performing product categories.
- Analyze customer purchasing patterns.
- Compare sales performance across different branches.
- Examine payment methods and customer preferences.
- Analyze sales trends over time.
- Identify key business KPIs and performance indicators.
- Present insights through clear and interactive Excel visualizations.

## Key Analysis Areas

### 1. Sales Performance
Analyzed overall sales performance to understand revenue generation and identify important sales patterns.

### 2. Product Category Analysis
Compared different product categories to identify categories contributing significantly to overall sales.

### 3. Branch Performance
Analyzed sales across different supermarket branches to understand variations in branch-level performance.

### 4. Customer Analysis
Examined customer-related patterns to understand purchasing behavior and customer segments.

### 5. Payment Method Analysis
Analyzed different payment methods to identify customer payment preferences.

### 6. Time-Based Analysis
Analyzed sales across different time periods to identify trends and variations in business performance.

## Data Analysis Process

The project followed a structured data analytics workflow:

1. Reviewed and understood the raw dataset.
2. Checked the data for missing values and inconsistencies.
3. Cleaned and prepared the dataset for analysis.
4. Created calculated fields and relevant KPIs.
5. Used Pivot Tables to summarize the data.
6. Created Pivot Charts for visual analysis.
7. Compared sales performance across multiple dimensions.
8. Identified meaningful trends and patterns.
9. Converted the analysis into actionable business insights.

The analysis converts raw supermarket transaction data into a structured analytical view of sales, products, customers, branches, and payment behavior.
