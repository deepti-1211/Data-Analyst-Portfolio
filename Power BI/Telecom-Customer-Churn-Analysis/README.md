# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

An interactive **Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard** developed in **Power BI** to analyze customer retention, churn patterns, service usage, contract types, tenure, and monthly charges.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key customer churn metrics and helps explore different customer segments through interactive visualizations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills

* Power BI
* DAX
* Data Analysis
* Data Visualization
* Dashboard Development
* KPI Analysis

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard includes analysis of:

* **Total Customers**
* **Churned Customers**
* **Churn Rate %**
* **Retained Customers**
* **Customer Risk Type**
* **Customer Retention Overview**
* **Contract-wise Churn**
* **Online Security & Online Backup**
* **Churn Rate by Tenure Band**
* **Average Monthly Charges – Churned vs Retained**
* **Monthly Charges by Customer Type**

## 🎯 Key Objectives

* Monitor overall customer churn and retention
* Analyze churn across different contract types
* Understand the relationship between tenure and churn
* Compare monthly charges for churned and retained customers
* Analyze customer risk categories
* Explore service-related patterns associated with customers

## 📈 Key Analysis Areas

### 👥 Customer Retention

Comparison of churned and retained customers using key KPIs and retention visuals.

### 📄 Contract Analysis

Analysis of customer churn across different contract categories.

### ⏳ Tenure Analysis

Churn rate is analyzed across different tenure bands to identify customer retention patterns.

### 💰 Monthly Charges

Comparison of average monthly charges between churned and retained customers.

### 🔐 Service Analysis

Customer services such as **Online Security** and **Online Backup** are analyzed along with monthly charges.

### 👤 Customer Type

Monthly charges are analyzed across different customer types.
